# Character engineering regression checks

The existing Character.tsx rig was not redrawn or altered in this follow-up. Tests compile that trusted repository TSX with TypeScript and render its static SVG through ReactDOMServer. No provider output or external attachment is executed.

- Assert the recorded blue #12bce5, yellow #ffda34 and outline #101719 palette, head center/radius116 and diameter232, torso height180, declared leg length150, black outer limb strokes48/50 and blue inner strokes32/34.
- Render all 12 expressions at frame45 in the point pose; require 12 distinct SVG fingerprints and match the frozen engineering baseline.
- Render all eight actions at frames0,12,24,45,75,105; require distinct body-geometry sequences. Walk/run/jump/fall/turn/hang must change actual transforms or limb paths; point/hang/sleep/fall also have explicit pose checks.
- Fingerprints are static fixtures and are not regenerated by tests. Intentional changes require an explicit baseline diff.

This is an engineering regression baseline, NOT owner approval, a reference-video SSIM comparison, exact appearance parity, or a claim that differently posed images can be meaningfully compared pixel-for-pixel. The source video and owner-supplied logo were not redrawn. Frame sheets from the actual 96-second incomplete render were inspected separately. Audio and word alignment are absent in that partial preview, and no completed narrated episode has passed acceptance.
